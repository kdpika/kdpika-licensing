# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. No harassment, discriminatory abuse,
threats, doxxing or publication of private customer/credential information.
Discuss code and evidence constructively; respect different experience levels.

Report conduct concerns privately to hi@kdpika.com. Maintainers may remove
harmful content or restrict participation proportionately and review context.
This project does not claim adoption of an external trademarked policy or a
response-time guarantee. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md instead.
